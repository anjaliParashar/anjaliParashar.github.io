About

Anjali Parashar
she/her · PhD Candidate
MIT MechE & AeroAstro, REALM Lab
Advisor: Prof. Chuchu Fan

My research develops principled methods for testing and evaluating autonomous systems. This involves efficiently finding diverse scenarios where they fail and quantifying behavior relative to human values through scenario generation. I leverage several Bayesian inference tools, such as Bayesian Experiment Design for user-in-the-loop design of scenarios and MCMC sampling combined with optimization for targeted exploration.

Previously, I finished my Master's thesis on optimization and control with Dr. Anuradha Annaswamy (2021–23), and Bachelor's thesis on fault-tolerant control of underwater vehicles under Dr. Santhakumar Mohan (2020).
